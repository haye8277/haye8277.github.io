How I got here

I'm a problem solver at heart, and the business always comes first. Pretty much every job I've had, I've ended up being the guy figuring out what's actually going wrong and what the best fix is. Sometimes that's a marketing change, sometimes it's a better process, and sometimes it's a script or a tool that makes things run smoother. Knowing a bit about how software gets built comes in handy more than you'd think.

Some of my favourite work happens between a good idea and a finished result: shaping it together with leadership, turning it into a plan people can get behind, and helping see it through to the end.

In 2017 I took a marketing job, and honestly it kind of snowballed from there. One role turned into a bunch of them: managing people, product development, ecommerce, social media, branding, keeping projects on track. A few highlights along the way: launching a product in front of around 1,000 people at a convention, growing three brands from scratch with no paid shortcuts, and helping a brand get through a pretty rough PR situation.

The message matters more than where you post it. I'm not big on fluff or vanity metrics. I just want to do stuff that actually works.the short version

Where the know-how comes from

I didn't start at the top. I worked my way up from the front lines, through sales and customer-facing roles, into creative and technical work, and eventually into leading marketing, teams and products. Every step taught me something I still use today.

  1. Front lines
  2. Sales
  3. Customer care
  4. Creative
  5. Technical
  6. Leadership{% include scribble-circle.svg %}

That mix is where the wide range of knowledge comes from. I understand the customer side, the creative side, the technical side and the business side, so when a problem cuts across a business, there's a good chance I've seen it from more than one angle.

None of that happened on my own. I've been lucky to work with a lot of people who were generous with what they knew, and I'm genuinely thankful to every one of them. A good part of what I bring to the table was passed along by them.seriously, thank you

What I'm up to now

Lately that's meant building some of the fixes myself. I've made tools to fix problems I kept running into at work, a sale manager and an analytics tool (more on those on the projects page). And just for fun, I'm building a video game on the side.

I lean on a lot of newer tools to build all of this. I'm not really into hype, but the practical stuff is pretty exciting. Things that would have taken me months are coming together in weeks.
