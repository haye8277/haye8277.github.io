01 How I got here

I'm a problem solver at heart, and the business always comes first. Pretty much every job I've had, I've ended up being the guy figuring out what's actually going wrong and what the best fix is. Sometimes that's a marketing change, sometimes it's a better process, and sometimes it's a script or a tool that makes things run smoother. Knowing a bit about how software gets built comes in handy more than you'd think.

A big part of what I do is working with executives. Someone has an idea in a meeting, and I'm the one who turns it into a real plan, gets the right people on it, and makes sure it actually ships. That gap between idea and execution is where I'm most useful.

In 2017 I took a marketing job, and honestly it kind of snowballed from there. One role turned into a bunch of them: managing people, product development, ecommerce, social media, branding, keeping projects on track. A few highlights along the way: launching a product in front of around 1,000 people at a convention, growing three brands from scratch with no paid shortcuts, and helping a brand get through a pretty rough PR situation.

The message matters more than where you post it. I'm not big on fluff or vanity metrics. I just want to do stuff that actually works.the short version

02 What I'm up to now

Lately that's meant building some of the fixes myself. I've made tools to fix problems I kept running into at work, a sale manager and an analytics tool (more on those on the projects page). I'm also working on a video game. Not saying much about that one yet.

I lean on a lot of newer tools to build all of this. I'm not really into hype, but the practical stuff is pretty exciting. Things that would have taken me months are coming together in weeks.

03 Music

When I need a break I make music as Knee Jar. It's weird electronic stuff, kind of dark and ambient, and honestly hard to describe. No training, no rules, I just mess around until something sounds cool. I've been playing with some newer production tools recently, which has taken things in a few directions I didn't expect. It's all up on SoundCloud if you want to check it out.
