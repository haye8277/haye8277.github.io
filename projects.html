Sale Manager

Scheduling and running sales across multiple online stores from a single screen.

Running a sale across several stores used to mean a spreadsheet, a lot of clicking, and a reminder to switch everything back. This app handles it end to end.

  • Connects to multiple stores, each in its own currency
  • Sale prices, automatic cart discounts or generated coupon codes
  • Scheduled start and end times in any time zone, applied and reverted automatically
  • Concurrent sales without conflicts
