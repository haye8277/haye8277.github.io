Sale Manager the one that started it all

Scheduled, store-wide discounts that show on the product, not just in the cart.

Most ecommerce platforms come with promotions built in, like buy one get one or spend-and-save offers. Those only kick in at the cart, once something's added or a condition is met. What they don't do well is a proper store-wide sale: set a specific discount across hundreds of products, show it right on the product page, and have it start and stop on schedule. That's what this does.

  • Percentage off, an amount off, or a set sale price
  • Applied in bulk, including straight from a CSV file
  • Shown on the product itself, not hidden until checkout
  • Scheduled to start and end automatically

Review Sharer no more copy and paste

The same reviews on every store, without copying them over by hand.

When the same products sell on more than one online store, each review stays stuck on the site it was written on. This app matches up the same products across the stores, even when each one sets them up differently and gives them its own product IDs. It then gathers every review in one place and works out what each store is missing. Before anything gets added, it checks for copies that are already there, tidies up the reviewer's name, and holds back anything that needs a second look.

  • Matches products across different setups and product IDs
  • Spots reviews already copied over, even with small edits
  • Sanitizes names, holding inappropriate ones back to check
  • Tries a single review first, and can undo anything it adds

Hue TV Sync movie nights, upgraded

Smart lights that follow what's on screen.

A very simple app for a more cinematic night in. It records a tiny copy of the TV screen and sends the colours to the Philips Hue lights around it, so the room reacts to whatever's playing.

  • Uses screen recording to read the picture
  • Works with the Hue lighting ecosystem
  • Updates the lights many times a second
  • One button: on or off
