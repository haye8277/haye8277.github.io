Sale Manager

Scheduled, store-wide discounts that show on the product, not just in the cart.

Most ecommerce platforms come with promotions built in, like buy one get one or spend-and-save offers. Those only kick in at the cart, once something's added or a condition is met. What they don't do well is a proper store-wide sale: set a specific discount across hundreds of products, show it right on the product page, and have it start and stop on schedule. That's what this does.

  • Percentage off, an amount off, or a set sale price
  • Applied in bulk, including straight from a CSV file
  • Shown on the product itself, not hidden until checkout
  • Scheduled to start and end automatically
