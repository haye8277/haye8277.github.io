Back in February I said I had some other things in the works. Well, it's been a busy few months, and a couple of those things are far enough along that I can actually talk about them. So here's a quick look at what's been taking up my evenings.

## A Sale Manager App

If you've ever run a sale across more than one online store, you know how tedious it gets. Different currencies, different time zones, hundreds of products, and someone has to remember to switch everything back the moment the sale ends. For a long time that someone was me, usually with a spreadsheet and an alarm on my phone.

So I built a tool to do it for me. It connects to multiple stores at once, and from one screen I can set up a sale and decide how it should work:

- **Sale prices** shown right on the product page
- **Automatic cart discounts** that apply at checkout, with no code needed
- **Coupon codes**, generated for me, for when a promo needs one

Every sale gets a start and end time in whatever time zone makes sense, and the app takes care of applying it and putting prices back when it's over. A few sales can run at the same time without stepping on each other, and each store keeps its own currency throughout.

It's not flashy, but it has already saved me hours and removed a whole category of "did I remember to turn that off?" worries.

## An Analytics Tool

The second project came from a similar frustration. When you're trying to understand how a business is doing, the data is everywhere. Sales numbers live on the ecommerce platform, website traffic is in Google Analytics, and social media has its own dashboards. Getting a clear picture meant hopping between tabs and stitching it together by hand.

The analytics tool pulls all of those sources into one place. Store data, site analytics and social media sit side by side, so I can see how a post or a campaign actually connects to traffic and sales instead of guessing. It's still growing, but it has already changed how I look at the numbers week to week.
