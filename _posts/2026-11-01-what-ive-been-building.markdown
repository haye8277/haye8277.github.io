Back in February I said I had some other things in the works. Well, it's been a busy few months, and a couple of those things are far enough along that I can actually talk about them. So here's a quick look at what's been taking up my evenings.

## A Sale Manager App

Most ecommerce platforms come with their own promotions built in: buy one get one free, spend a certain amount and save, that kind of thing. The catch is they all work at the cart level. The discount only shows up once something's been added to the cart, or once a customer meets some condition like buying a certain number of items.

What they don't handle well is a proper store-wide sale. Setting a specific discount across hundreds of products, having it show right on the product page where customers actually see it, and having it start and stop on a schedule. For a long time that meant a spreadsheet, a lot of clicking, and an alarm on my phone to switch everything back.

So I built a tool to do it for me. I can set up a sale using:

- **A percentage off**, like 20% off everything in a collection
- **A set amount off**, like $10 off each product
- **A specific sale price** for each product

It all works in bulk, including straight from a CSV file, so a sale across hundreds of products is as easy as one. Every sale gets a start and end time, and the app takes care of applying it and putting prices back when it's over.

It's not flashy, but it has already saved me hours and removed a whole category of "did I remember to turn that off?" worries.

## An Analytics Tool

The second project came from a similar frustration. Every platform gives you its own reports, but they only go so deep. Sales numbers live on the ecommerce platform, website traffic is in Google Analytics, and social media has its own dashboards, and none of them let you really dig in.

The analytics tool is about reviewing that data properly. It brings it together so I can compare performance across a whole range of factors, track the actual return on investment for campaigns and spend, and analyze the data we already have in more depth than the built-in reports allow. It's still growing, but it has already changed how I look at the numbers week to week.

## And... a Video Game

This one is just for fun. I've been building a video game in Unity as a passion project.

It's a third-person game with a heavy focus on large-scale battles and strategy. There's no big plan behind it, it's just something I enjoy chipping away at, and it has eaten more late nights than I'd like to admit.

## Building With New Tools

One thing all three projects have in common is that I leaned heavily on modern development tools to build them. I like the practical side of new technology more than the hype, and these projects are a good example. Things that would have taken me months on my own came together in weeks, and I learned a lot along the way.

I'll keep posting updates here as things progress. Thanks for reading!
